Marketing Plan

Showing up
with intention.

This plan covers July through December 2026 — a pivotal window as the Deloitte engagement closes and The Greer Collective steps fully into its own market presence. The goal is not volume for its own sake. It is strategic visibility: the right people understanding who Jacqueline Greer is, what The Greer Collective does, and why it is exactly what they need. JacquieVoice, the creative and voice work brand, is a distinct and separate expression of the same person — same DNA, different room — and is not the subject of this plan.

Brand Foundation

Two brands. One woman.

The Greer Collective
Executive Leadership Practice — This Plan
The consulting, facilitation, and coaching arm. Jacqueline shows up here as a strategist, a thought partner, and a practitioner with over 20 years of VP-level experience in education, nonprofit, and government. This is where organizations come when they are navigating change, building leadership capacity, or developing the next generation of leaders.
thegreercollective.com · jacqueline@thegreercollective.com · LinkedIn
JacquieVoice
Creative & Voice Work — Separate Brand
The narration, voice acting, and creative expression arm. Jacqueline shows up here as an artist, bringing purpose-driven content to life through professional voice work. JacquieVoice has its own platforms, its own audience, and its own market positioning. The two brands share DNA — not space.
jacquievoice.com · Voice123 · Voices.com · Not featured in this plan
North Star

By December 2026, The Greer Collective has a warm post-Deloitte pipeline, an active coaching roster, a clear content presence on LinkedIn and Substack, and is known in its markets as the practice you call when you want leadership development done with rigor and genuine care.

Target Audiences

Who this is for.

Primary
Government & Federal Agencies
Program officers, HR leads, and L&D directors at federal agencies and contractors building leadership development programs. The Deloitte/USPS engagement is the proof of concept. These buyers value credentialed facilitators with deep sector fluency.
Primary
Nonprofit & Education Institutions
Executive directors, chiefs of staff, and board members at education nonprofits, foundations, and mission-driven organizations. Jacqueline's VP-level experience makes her fluent in the complexity and constraints of this sector.
Secondary
Women Leaders in Transition
Senior professionals navigating pivots, promotions, or purpose shifts who want a coach with real organizational experience. The coaching pillar serves this audience directly through 1:1 engagements and cohort models.
Content Strategy

The Whole Self content arc.

Pillar One · LinkedIn
The Practitioner's View
Short-form professional posts rooted in what Jacqueline is actually doing: facilitation insights, leadership patterns she notices in rooms, reflections on the practice. Two to three posts per month. Not thought leadership performance — real observations from real work.
Pillar Two · Substack / Website
The Whole Self Series
Monthly long-form essays rotating across The Greer Collective practice, JacquieVoice creative life, and Jacqueline as a person. These are the foundation of the future book on the Thriving Framework. One essay per month, cross-posted to thegreercollective.com and Substack.
Pillar Three · Warm Outreach
Personal Relationship Maintenance
Direct, personal outreach to existing contacts and warm prospects — not cold email blasts. Check-ins, coffee conversations, forwarding relevant resources. Fifteen to twenty intentional touches per month across the network.
Pillar Four · Speaking & Presence
Visible in the Right Rooms
Panels, conference appearances, IAF community participation, board engagement. Physical and professional presence in spaces where the right people gather. One to two visibility moments per quarter minimum.

A note on pace and sustainability. This plan is designed to be executable by one person without a team — because the work itself is the priority. No tactic here requires more than a few hours per week. The rhythm is consistent, not exhausting. Content emerges from the work that is already happening. Outreach comes from real relationships, not cold lists. The goal is a practice that grows because of who Jacqueline is and what The Greer Collective delivers, not because of volume or performance.

The Thriving Framework is not just the subject of a future book. It is the filter this plan runs through. Every decision here was checked against one question: does this protect the thriving, or does it trade it for something else?
