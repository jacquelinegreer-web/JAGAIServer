Cream on

  • Page body background
  • Light text on dark sections
  • Form backgrounds & cards
  • Type specimen backgrounds
  • Alternating sections

Lavender is light — never use it for small text on white or cream. Use it as a fill, border, or background, and place Taupe or Near-Black text on top. The Lavender + Taupe pairing is the fixed JacquieVoice signature.
