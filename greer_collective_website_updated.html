About

The work is
always about
the people.

Greer Collective is the executive leadership consulting and coaching practice of Jacqueline Greer — a leadership development strategist with over 20 years of VP-level experience in education, nonprofit, and government sectors.

Whether facilitating a leadership cohort of USPS executives at the Bolger Center, coaching a leader navigating a pivotal career transition, or designing a flagship program from the ground up, the approach is always the same: build the conditions for people to do their best collective work.

Jacqueline's practice is grounded in adaptive leadership, adult learning theory, and equity-centered facilitation — and shaped by VP-level experience at Equal Opportunity Schools and Urban Teachers (City Teaching Alliance), where she co-built and led programs that changed lives at scale.

Jacqueline also works as a professional narrator and voice artist under JacquieVoice — a separate creative brand. Same woman, same DNA, a different room entirely.

Our Approach

Process
is the
product.

Greer Collective doesn't arrive with prescribed answers. The work begins by creating the conditions — the right room, the right sequence, the right questions — that allow individuals and organizations to surface what they already know and move toward what they're ready to become.

This is grounded practice, not theory. Every engagement is designed around the specific group, goal, and moment at hand.

01
Human-centered
People are not problems to be managed. They are the source of every organization's greatest capacity. The work starts there.
02
Equity-grounded
Power dynamics, access, and lived experience shape every room. Effective facilitation and coaching account for what's in the room — and what isn't.
03
Rigorously adaptive
Plans are starting points. What actually happens in the room — the questions that surface, the energy that shifts — is where real work gets done.
04
Built for transfer
The measure of any engagement is what participants carry back into their organizations. Everything is designed with sustainable change as the destination.
